Products & projects

Founder project

KLK Invoice Management System

A logistics invoicing system developed by Chrisfivo Sutarto, the founder of Teshy Labs. It supports transaction tracking, digital signatures, PDF export, and recoverable deletion. It is built with AI-assisted development.

Teshy Labs product

Kassentix

Kassentix is a cloud-based point of sale product by Teshy Labs for Indonesian businesses, with sales and transaction management, inventory synchronization, and multi-outlet management.

Built with Claude

Claude is used for AI-assisted development, including work on KLK Invoice Management System and Kassentix. In Kassentix, Claude also powers AI analytics within the product.
